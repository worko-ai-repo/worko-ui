How it works
Employee Activation:

Encourage your employees to be the driving force behind your talent acquisition strategy. Activate them on Worko.ai to explore and recommend the best-suited candidates for your openings.

Smart Candidate Requests:

Job seekers express their interest and ask for referrals. Worko.ai's matching algorithm meticulously analyzes CVs, allowing only the most suitable candidates to request referrals.

Employee Referral Portal:

Your employees act as the gatekeepers. They review, select, and send CVs to your hiring manager or internal referral portal, ensuring that only top-tier candidates proceed.

Action and Feedback Loop:

Companies take decisive actions on referred candidates and share valuable feedback with Worko and the referring employees. It's a collaborative process aimed at building a robust talent pipeline.

Benefits for Corporate
Ultra-Filtered CVs:

Enjoy a three-tier filtering system that ensures every CV reaching your hiring manager has been rigorously vetted by Worko.ai's AI algorithm, undergone background verification, and received input from your employees/referees.

Junk CV Elimination:

Referees are incentivized to maintain a high referral standard with a limit of six referrals per month, ensuring quality over quantity. Ratings based on referral success further enhance CV quality.

Employee Motivation:

Boost employee morale with a referral bonus system, fostering active participation and engagement in the hiring process.

Significant Time Savings:

With all roles undergoing three-level filtration, companies can save up to 70% of manhours typically spent on interviewing multiple candidates for a single position.

Accelerated Hiring Timeline:

Benefit from a streamlined process that significantly reduces the hiring timeline without compromising on quality.
