Job Description
Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) Officer

The Monitoring and Evaluation Officer will be responsible for developing and implementing a results-based management framework to enhance the organization's capacity to report effectively on program outcomes. The officer will monitor and ensure high-quality, timely inputs while maintaining the strategic vision and ensuring project activities achieve intended outputs in a cost-effective and timely manner.

Roles & Responsibilities:
  • . Develop/adopt a plan for project-related support supervision in M&E for partners.
  • . Oversee data collection, collation, storage, analysis, and reporting, ensuring data quality.
  • . Manage research activities and ensure outcomes and lessons learned are shared with relevant stakeholders.
  • . Develop project M&E framework, including baseline, mid-term, and impact assessments.
  • . Facilitate and serve as project liaison for externally-managed evaluations.
  • . Organize field visits to verify data provided in reports.
  • . Provide technical support to project partners and organize training on M&E as required.
Qualification and Skills:
  • . At least 2-3 years of experience in designing and implementing project M&E systems.
  • . Knowledge of major evaluation methodologies (qualitative, quantitative, mixed-method).
  • . Experience with data collection, analysis, and report production.
